Bluetooth controlled motor. You can input motor speed and direction through your phone. L293B H-Bridge controlled is used in order to control the motor. Accelerometer detects if the board is upright, working as a safety mechanism, and then allows the motor to work. 4 LEDs indicate the input motor speed.
